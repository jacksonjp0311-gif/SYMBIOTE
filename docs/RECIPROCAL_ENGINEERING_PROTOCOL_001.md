## Operational question and authorization

Can a configured native participant learn partial exact graph knowledge from
two explicitly authorized endpoints and return an inert, independently useful
map to each, with the receiving owners retaining control over evaluation and
adoption? Run on this PC only. No model, external agent or third-party truth
authority is used. The truth is an operator-pinned finite local fixture.

Use three private native owners: central A and partners B/C. B initially learns
scope X through actual metered graph discovery; C similarly learns scope Y.
All comparison arms include the same source-discovery cost. A acquires and
independently verifies both maps. Its configured cognitive episode offers the
other scope to each partner. B/C quarantine the offers. Each receiving owner is
stopped, independently evaluates through its local CLI, and may reject or adopt.
Actual process restarts must preserve nonempty correct predictions and identity.
A also restarts with zero fresh promotions. No executable artifact is transferred.

An operator must explicitly opt into a bounded reciprocal **experiment** in the
new local truth/task contract and existing connection/resource grants. This
permits a finite uncertain-value inert offer; it does not fabricate qualified
positive sender utility or override the ordinary negative-value policy. Record
EXPERIMENT, uncertainty, every offer and its budget charge before the payload.
No partner's bare receipt or content digest becomes verified benefit. The usual
full-fixture version-1 affine and graph policies remain conservative.

## Declared task family and cohort

Each scope has four parallel two-edge paths from node 0 through four branch
nodes to node 5, plus the relay 5→6: seven nodes and nine directed edges. Schema
topology and intervals 1..9 are public; absent edges are explicitly structural.
One branch is cheap, with both costs 1. Each other branch has first-edge cost 9;
its second edge remains in the declared interval. Relay cost is 1. Never treat
a missing transferred observation as a known weight. Exact RouteLearner bounds
must certify every answer; hints cannot silently shrink unknown intervals.

Source training queries are 0→5 and 5→6. The receiver's distinct held-out target
is 0→6. The partial map identifies the cheap branch and relay, three observed
edges. The receiver must independently look up those three costs and obtain an
unchanged exact conditional certificate, without inspecting other oracle values
to choose its strategy. A wrong cost or incompatible schema must be rejected.

The registered engineering cohort enumerates all 16 pairs of cheap-branch
positions for scopes X/Y, two deterministic labelings (identity and a declared
branch permutation), and oracle prices 1 and 100,000: 64 paired cells. Record
the exact permutation/generator in the registration before any cell opens.
These finite cells and remappings are related controls, not independent samples.
Generic unit/smoke fixtures must be identified separately and cannot count as
execution of this registered cohort.

## Controls and accounting

For each cell and each participant report the isolated native greedy policy,
all 24 fixed branch-order policies selected without target truth, equal-query
isolated exploration, a matched-total-resource isolated control, and reciprocal
transfer. Also retain an incompatible/wrong-map rejection control. No per-world
hindsight choice is an available selector; report that bound separately if used.
Every control uses the identical topology, task, prior bounds and oracle price.

The isolated greedy target uses three queries plus one for each preceding
expensive first edge. The balanced prior mean is 4.5 queries; independent map
verification uses three. This is a conditional pre-exchange prior, not a promise
of gain in every cell. First-ranked cheap branches tie in oracle calls and lose
after overhead. Low-price transfers may lose. Preserve and report all losses.

Meter each actual local truth lookup; retain query/order/outcome traces. Record
exact learner work, actual plaintext bytes for every negotiation/RPC/offer and
response, adoption, rejection, source acquisition and restart work. Convert
oracle calls using the predeclared price, and count each measured non-oracle
work unit and plaintext byte as one declared unit; no free sender work or wire.
Hardware time and cryptographic wire overhead are separate diagnostics. Report
each participant independently as well as total experiment resource consumption.

Counterfactual/control execution must have separate private learner state and
must not leak its discoveries into an exchange participant. Distinguish
per-arm operational task costs from the total cost of running and auditing all
controls. Do not call a task-cost saving a net profit for the whole research
campaign. Any extra oracle calls performed by operational verification belong
to the exchange arm, even if they make its result negative.

## Acceptance

Operational PASS requires actual configured A→B/C payloads; exact independent
receiver acceptance or explicit rejection; at least one correctly extended
partner capability from an adopted return; zero incorrect certificates; durable
nonempty retained predictions after real OS restarts; and complete independently
checked provenance, costs, grants and resource limits. Correctly declining a
full-fixture offer alone cannot satisfy it. A fabricated receipt cannot pass.

Report conditional task utility separately for each cell/control and aggregate.
Do not require or claim all cells improve; equality, loss and matched-budget
explanations remain visible. Failure to beat the strongest available fixed or
matched-resource control is NOT_DEMONSTRATED for that efficiency comparison,
even if operational reciprocal contribution passes. No generalized transfer,
unfamiliar-agent selection or autonomous-improvement claim follows.

HOLD applies to missing evidence, interruption, resource exhaustion, changed
source/registration/binary, unauthorized scope, or any incorrect certificate.
Use the existing bounded process containment and a predeclared whole-cohort
envelope of 1,200 seconds, 128 MiB receipts, and no automatic retry. Preserve the
completed prefix and do not raise a failed budget. Retain historical failures.
