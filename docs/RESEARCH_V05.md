# v0.5: prospective relationships, with a failed primary preserved

The first registered primary remains **HOLD**. A subsequent **engineering
reproduction** completed all 192 cases using the same 24 graph worlds, original
empty identities and opaque context labels. It demonstrates bounded operation,
conditional reasoning and persistent state in this laboratory. It supplies no
new independent sample and does not change the primary's status.

At the normal observation price, prospective A+B1 aggregate cost fell relative
to isolation, with both agents benefiting in 18 of 24 worlds. It did not beat
the fixed-peer control, improve held-out
coverage, or demonstrate a change in selected actions caused by prior profiles.
All losses and abstentions remain in the evidence.

## Method

The cohort contains six graph configurations for each of 5, 6, 7 and 8 nodes:
24 positive-cost DAGs with at most 28 edges. Generation, physical edge ordering,
uniform-control draws and the topology uniqueness rule are fixed by the protocol.
Both price regimes use those same worlds. Four arms—isolated, fixed peer,
uniform authorized peer and prospective frontier—produce 192 cases in total.
This count does not represent 192 independent environments.

Each arm/price group has five separately supervised native learner processes
and a separate observation authority. A and B1 receive complementary warmups;
B2 supplies redundant information; B3 has an incompatible domain and stays
unprobed; B4 offers a genuine observation from a previous context as a stale
control. An unauthorized descriptor has no endpoint. Dijkstra and bounded
simple-path enumeration alternate by the fixed role/world assignment. A separate
authority checks authenticated edge observations and route claims against frozen
shortest-path labels. No language model participates.

Pairing preserves five logical learner identities across the eight
counterfactual groups, with separate files, journals, TLS grants and authority
epochs. The resulting layout has **49 private state paths and 14 logical
identities**: 40 learner paths, eight authorities and one controller. Of the
40 learner profiles, 32 are active and eight belong to idle B3 controls. They
share one laboratory/controller and the same worlds; the 32 active profiles are
not independent research teams. Fresh reproductions restore only validated,
genesis-only ledger bytes and public context mappings. They import no models,
outcomes, private keys or old receipt authority. Authentication material is fresh.

The prospective policy considers the current unknown route frontier before
requesting evidence. Its exact rational partner estimate is
`(verified_novel + 1) / (attempts + 2)`; two verification/applicability failures
quarantine a partner. Scores do not authorize access or make a receipt trusted.
Every received observation still needs an authorized source, matching schema and
context, an authentic current authority receipt and a safe native state update.

Training comprises the main route and the public warmup edge query, deduplicated
when identical. Every other reachable ordered query is held out. Conditional
certificates must follow from observed edge costs and the remaining declared
intervals. Unsupported routes abstain. A durable graph map is promoted only after
every predeclared held-out query passes independent assessment. A fixed process
restart after world 11 tests private profile continuity; final fresh-process
replay tests retained models without buying or verifying more observations.

The declared participant bill is:

```text
P × observation purchase attempts
+ API requests + 2 × sessions
+ ceil(sent application bytes / 1024) + ceil(received application bytes / 1024)
+ 2 × receipt inspection attempts + adopted edges + route queries
+ native model work + ceil(exported bytes / 1024)
```

Application bytes include Hello/Agreement negotiation and RPC JSONL frames.
Advertisements, source warmups, failed purchase/inspection attempts, both exchange
participants and adoption are charged. Participant limits remain 128 sessions,
256 requests, 1 MiB application traffic, 100,000 native work units, at most one
purchase per edge and three exchange attempts per world. Operations retain their
two-second timeout, worlds their 30-second cap and the campaign its 1,800-second
cap. Transferred content is inert structured evidence.

Controller/judge activity, TLS records, OS processes, audit I/O and offline replay
validation are outside the participant utility bill and reported separately.
Logical savings establish no wall-clock, CPU, energy or monetary speedup.

## Results, including losses

1,596**, abstained on **1,028**, and issued **zero incorrect conditional
certificates**. Thus held-out coverage was approximately 35.6%, with no coverage
advantage over isolation. Each arm also completed its 96 main routes. Repeated
queries across paired arms and prices do not add independent samples.

## Retention and operational profile effects

There were **64 stored promotions across 32 active private learner profiles**.
They cover only **two distinct learned task contexts**:

| World index | Nodes | Context ID |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 5 | `frontier-context-986c0dcb0864593f12c3947e639a5add093af335b1e4368871fa4adc4b401bcf` |
| 7 | 6 | `frontier-context-d328321925332832fe54dcc3808699a727e6676e9f3e7e96eb0f680ddad42b4b` |

These are repeated private promotions of two contexts, not 64 independent
discoveries. Every active learner retained two validated models and produced
21 certified offline predictions in final fresh-process replay. Every group
retained two common A/B1 contexts. Actual observation, verification and assessment
counters were unchanged across replay. Isolation retained the same two contexts;
retention alone therefore does not establish an advantage caused by exchange.

Normal prospective A made 102 decisions with prior completed profiles. Eight
post-restart decisions had a changed score; **zero selected actions changed**.
B1 recorded 110 such decisions and twelve post-restart score effects, also with
zero action changes. The journals establish durable operational score use. They
do not demonstrate a behavioral improvement caused by that history. The cheap
prospective profiles recorded no exchange outcomes or corresponding effects.

## Runtime and resource scope

The engineering reproduction took **1,206.891 seconds**, including
**1,082.8926714 seconds of measured parent admission waiting**. Its shared
50 ms minimum parent-session spacing admitted 23,680 attempts with zero admission
rejections and no connection retries. Native shutdown witnesses record 29,092
accepted connections/application requests and 128 stopped owned child instances.
Nested learner calls remain separately bounded; the admission limiter does not
claim a global 20-connections-per-second TCP rate.

On the recorded Windows x86-64 host, owned child CPU times summed to
374.921875 seconds and controller CPU time was 37 seconds. The largest individual
child OS-reported peak working set was 14,405,632 bytes (13.74 MiB). Controller
peak working set at receipt capture was 92,340,224 bytes (88.06 MiB); that capture
precedes final serialization and is not a complete lifetime peak. Individual
process peaks are not simultaneous aggregate system memory. Pacing fixes resource
admission at the cost of waiting; this is not speedup evidence.

## Verification and release evidence

The [v0.5 evidence index](../experiments/v0.5/README.md) lists immutable bindings,
planned release archives and exact raw-verification commands. The independent
checker validates the full registry, arithmetic, route labels, history bindings,
receipt applicability, private profiles, setup restoration and native replay
witnesses. A separate original-to-engineering audit checks the 136 comparable
completed cases. Primitive traffic/process counters remain pinned native
witnesses; there was no independent packet capture or arbitrary remote-truth
attestation.

Software/safety and bounded retained-state criteria passed in the engineering
run. The original primary qualification remains HOLD; better selection than both
relationship controls, profile-induced action improvement, increased coverage,
cross-domain transfer and general intelligence remain unestablished. The next
research question requires a new preregistered design and unseen environments,
with the observed fixed-peer advantage and cheap-price losses retained as
counterexamples.
