## Question

Can two agents exchange a structural constraint, after the receiver certifies it
with its own evidence, and both finish fresh held-out tasks at strictly lower
declared cost than equal-budget isolated learning and a fixed strategy, without
an incorrect certificate?

## Participants and arms

Use two independent SYMBIOTE owners and at least one compatible learner that
does not share the SYMBIOTE query or hint implementation. Compare:

- isolated learning
- equal-budget isolated exploration
- one-way transfer
- reciprocal transfer
- a fixed strategy chosen without the partner's state
- a strategy learned only from the local source tasks
- exchanged structural knowledge
- an incorrect or mismatched constraint

Held-out tasks are generated after this protocol is pinned. They are not the
16 labelled graphs from protocol 001. Remapped labels, a wrong family and a
restart with zero replay oracle calls are required controls.

## Receipts and costs

Every receiver writes a `symbiote.reciprocal-evaluation/2` receipt. This
wording replaces `symbiote.reciprocal-evaluation/1` before any target for this
protocol is opened. A content digest is not benefit. Benefit requires a
promotion between the receiver's own before and after anchors, adoption and a
behavior change derived from that promotion, and the stored exchange total
strictly below the stored isolated baseline. Delivery, quarantine, agreement,
and an unfinished promotion do not satisfy it. Durable benefit additionally
requires the restart flag. Research success requires two such receipts with
crossed identities and the same transferred record; a caller-supplied sender
net on one receipt is not the other participant's result. Scores in different
units are not added. The acceptance table below is unchanged. Status remains
**NOT_RUN**.

A transferred constraint may choose an experiment. It may not remove a
hypothesis or authorize a prediction until the receiver's own observations do.
The exact affine and graph certification rules stay in force.

## Acceptance

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| PASS | Both nets are positive after transfer, verification and regression costs; incorrect certificates are zero; both owners retain a nonempty prediction after a real restart with zero replay oracle calls; the mismatched arm is rejected or revoked without deleting history |
| NOT_DEMONSTRATED | The run completes and any success condition above fails |
| HOLD | The run is incomplete, over budget, or its source, binary or registration pin changed |

Repeated tasks and remapped copies are not independent samples. A negative net
stays negative. This protocol does not close the hour campaign, the two-host
gate, or stable v1 publication.
