# Path to a stable bounded SYMBIOTE

The stable target is an embeddable Rust agent with exact reasoning, reciprocal
exchange and evaluated persistent knowledge in its declared affine and graph
families. Stable software does not establish general intelligence, arbitrary
remote truth or universal transfer efficiency. The original version objectives
remain in [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md); unsuccessful research criteria stay visible.

## Preserve the prospective study and unproven criteria

The protocol-004 primary remains HOLD, with every failure and unfinished case
preserved. The later engineering reproduction completed the same 24 worlds and
all 192 control cases with the original setup; it is not a new independent sample.
The fixed-peer control remains cheaper, and changed scores caused no changed
actions in that trace. Better selection and unfamiliar-partner generalization
are not established. Future studies need new prospectively frozen tasks and
acceptance criteria; do not retune or replace the completed cohort.

The predeclared nonvacuity rule requires every group to
preserve all accepted models, profiles and authority counters across restart.
The positive persistent-learning summary additionally requires at least one
shared validated retained context for A and B1 in the normal-price prospective
group at final restart, correct nonempty offline predictions for each, zero
observe/verify/assess calls during replay, and at least one post-midpoint decision
by central A that uses its prior durable completed outcomes. B1's profile-use
count is reported separately. Empty controls are reported separately. Equality of two
empty model lists establishes no learned capability. This evidence rule changes
neither the frozen policy, cohort, prices nor protocol 004's utility criteria.

## Maintain identities and trust within the checked envelope

v0.5.1 implements an offline, explicit policy transition anchored to the previous
policy digest and operational journal head. It preserves owner identity and prior
history, requires coordinated downtime for rotation, and keeps denials unless
the operator explicitly regrants access. Restrictive changes settle old intents
before recovery can promote them.

Local tests cover old-pin rejection, retained identity, denial persistence,
stale transitions, rejected unreviewed widening and bounded recovery/HOLD paths.
The continuity campaign alternates two preprovisioned server leaves four times;
it does not demonstrate automatic renewal, remote administration or every
interruption under arbitrary deployment conditions.

## Make durable state portable and reproducible

Verified offline backup/restore now includes identity, knowledge, operational
registration, deny history, idempotent receipts and layout metadata, with file
hashes and new-destination isolation. Private keys are excluded and activation
dependencies are declared. Each configured restore requires explicit review,
including a copied relative configuration or unchanged policy digest. Separate
genesis-only laboratory setup restoration reproduces opaque identity ordering
without importing learned outcomes.

Observed checks include exact restored identity/history/predictions, persistent
denials and receipts, rejected altered or missing files, interrupted restore
isolation and logical replay from recorded simulation setup. These cover a
stopped single owner and the declared same-OS format; they do not qualify
cross-OS restore, network filesystems or power-loss durability. Do not
promise byte-identical timings, cryptographic nonces or operating-system counters.

## Qualify one bounded release candidate

The fixed S5 campaign covers both manual task families, owned native processes,
local TLS, a genuinely lost committed quarantine reply, one killed-and-waited
service, explicit rotations and verified relocation. Manual checks and named
regressions additionally exercise drift, negative transfers and resource bounds.
The pending third quarantine is rejected during relocation; it does not become
a new learned model. No generic claim of autonomous repair follows.

Before declaring stable v1.0, preserve a separately declared longer-duration
failure/recovery envelope, qualify the supported API/format/platform window and
verify installation/restart from the actual published assets. The v0.5.1 local
campaign is one completed bounded gate, not a substitute for these remaining
checks or the scientific criteria above.

Secure configured APIs on one host are distinct from independent multi-host
deployment evidence. Missing deployment evidence remains an open gate rather
than a claim inferred from accepting a non-loopback address.

Larger networks, online enrollment, automatic renewal, new task families and
broader learning advantages are subsequent research. They must not be implied
by a stable release of the bounded core.
